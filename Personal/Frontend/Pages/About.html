SO WHAT IS THIS?

This website is my own little patch of the Internet. It is not a company, not a portfolio trying desperately to impress somebody, and certainly not a carefully optimized personal brand.

It is simply a place where I can put things. Thoughts. Projects. Pictures. Writing. Experiments. Random nonsense that seemed important at three in the morning.

The Internet used to feel like this. You found someone's strange little website, clicked around, discovered a page you weren't expecting, and somehow ended up reading it for twenty minutes.

This website exists because I think that feeling should still exist.

ABOUT THE WEBMASTER

I write things. I make things. I collect ideas, occasionally finish them, and occasionally leave them abandoned in a digital ditch.

I like old websites, strange designs, writing, machines, computers, roads, photographs, and anything else that manages to hold my attention for longer than five minutes.

This corner is where all of those interests are allowed to collide without a committee meeting first.

WHY DOES IT LOOK LIKE THIS?

Because I like it.

Modern websites are wonderful, technically impressive, extremely fast, beautifully responsive, and frequently indistinguishable from one another.

I wanted something that felt handmade. Something with visible borders, strange little boxes, tiny text, unnecessary counters, and enough green to make a forest jealous.

So this is what happened.
